An interactive visualization of the Manhattan (L1) distance metric. Explore how the sum of absolute differences defines geometry, paths, and neighborhoods in 2D and higher dimensions.

About This Visualization

  • Start point (A)
  • End point (B)

Drag the points or use the reset button.

Drag the blue (A) and red (B) points. The yellow path animates the shortest axis-aligned route. Gray lines show Δx and Δy. The L1 neighborhood is the green diamond. Try snapping to the grid for clarity.

What is Manhattan Distance?

The Manhattan distance (also called L1 norm or taxicab distance) between two points is the sum of the absolute differences of their coordinates. In 2D, it measures how far you would travel along a grid of city blocks, never moving diagonally.

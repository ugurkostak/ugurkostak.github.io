Spirography

Published: June 2026

An interactive visualization of spirography patterns, exploring the mathematical beauty of hypotrochoid and epitrochoid curves generated by rolling circles within or around other circles.

About This Visualization

  • Interactive Spirography
  • Parametric Curves

Adjust the parameters to generate different spirography patterns. Interact with the canvas to explore the infinite variety of curves created by rolling circles.

Interaction: Use controls to adjust outer radius, inner radius, and pen distance. Watch the patterns evolve in real time.

Spirography is a geometric visualization that creates mesmerizing patterns through the mathematical principle of rolling circles. Whether you're drawing with a spirograph toy or computing hypotrochoid equations, the result is always surprising: simple rules generate endlessly varied, beautiful curves.
